GTA 6 Shorts Studio

Personal editing tool for a single creator.

GTA 6 Shorts Studio is a private tool I use to prepare short vertical videos about GTA 6. Each video is edited (selected moment, captions, hook, 9:16 reframing, permanent on-screen credit and a link to the original channel), then reviewed and approved by me before anything is uploaded.

With my approval, the tool sends the video to my own TikTok inbox as a draft (video.upload) or to my own YouTube channel as a private video. I finish and publish every post myself, in the official apps.

It has no other users, no public sign-up, and it does not collect data from anyone.
