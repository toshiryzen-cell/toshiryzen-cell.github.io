Privacy Policy

GTA 6 Shorts Studio · Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Summary

GTA 6 Shorts Studio is a single-user tool. It does not collect, sell or share personal data from anyone. The only accounts it connects to are my own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

2. Data the tool handles

  • OAuth tokens for my own TikTok and YouTube accounts (access token, refresh token, expiry, granted scope, TikTok open_id). They are stored only on my own computer, in a file readable only by the tool, and are never logged or shared.
  • The videos I approve, which are uploaded to my TikTok inbox (as drafts) or to my YouTube channel (as private videos).

The tool does not read followers, comments, messages, contacts, analytics or any data of other TikTok or YouTube users.

3. This website

This website has no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. The sign-in return page only shows the address in your own browser so I can copy it; it sends nothing to any server.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until I disconnect the tool. I can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions) and in my Google account (Security > Third-party apps), and delete the local token file.

5. Third parties

Uploads go only to TikTok and Google (YouTube), under their own privacy policies.
